# Earth asset sources

- Geographic land/coastline polygons: **Natural Earth**, `ne_50m_land`, 1:50 million geographic dataset. Source mirror: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson
- Original download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson
- Natural Earth terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Natural Earth vector and raster map data are in the public domain.
- `earth_surface_2048.png`: generated by Silicon LogiX from those polygons. Coastlines are geographic; surface colours are an original, illustrative biome palette, not an elevation/satellite photograph or measurement.
- `earth_clouds_2048.png`: original reproducible synthetic noise-based partial cloud cover, not observed/live weather.
- Resolution of both texture rasters: 2048 × 1024, equirectangular; north at image top, longitude −180° at image left.
- Download and generated texture date: 2026-10-12.

## Interpretation

Earth is rendered with display radius 8 units and centre `(0, 0, -10.3)`. It is deliberately not in the same metre-scale coordinate system as the spacecraft engineering model. Its geographic orientation is `R_x(-20°) @ R_z(-72°)`.

The Emilia `(44.77° N, 11.49° E)` and Matera `(40.65° N, 16.70° E)` markers are illustrative project locations. Their names/coordinates imply neither a partner organisation nor operational ground stations. Decorative orbit and dotted-link annotations in the original GLB are hidden during mission rendering; the live trajectory follows the recorded simulator state.
